# Data Structures & Algorithms Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Algorithms_ (4th Edition) by Robert Sedgewick & Kevin Wayne](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/)
  Foundational text with visual step-by-step traces and empirical runtime plots. Use for: core data structures, sorting algorithms, and graph traversal.
- [Book: _Introduction to Algorithms_ (CLRS) by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, & Stein](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262046305/introduction-to-algorithms/)
  Authoritative reference for formal definitions of asymptotic bounds, recurrences, and algorithmic correctness.
- [Course: MIT 6.006 Introduction to Algorithms](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-spring-2020/)
  High-signal university lecture series focusing on algorithmic thinking, divide-and-conquer, dynamic programming, and data structure invariants.
- [Book: _Grokking Algorithms_ by Aditya Bhargava](https://www.manning.com/books/grokking-algorithms)
  Visual, beginner-friendly guide that builds intuition through illustrated analogies before code.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/leetcode & NeetCode Community](https://reddit.com/r/leetcode)
  High-activity peer community analyzing modern technical interview problems, pattern recognition, and real-time trade-off discussions.
- [Hacker News & Engineering Blogs (High Scalability)](http://highscalability.com/)
  Real-world post-mortems and architecture breakdowns showing where algorithmic bottlenecks triggered production failures.
