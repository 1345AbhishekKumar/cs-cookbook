Backend engineering is the discipline of architecting **reliable, fault-tolerant, scalable, secure, and maintainable distributed computer systems**. 

Rather than viewing backends through the narrow lens of any single framework or language, this track builds language-agnostic mental models from the physical hardware up to Layer 7 protocols.

<Callout type="info" title="Networking & Systems Foundations">
To dive even deeper into low-level operating system scheduling and raw packet transport, check out:
- **[Computer Networking](/docs/computer-networking)** for OSI layer frames and TCP handshakes.
- **[Operating Systems](/docs/operating-system)** for processes, threads, virtual memory, and kernel mode.
</Callout>
