Welcome to **Stage 3: Layer 3 & Routing**. 

Layer 2 switching delivers frames between neighbors on the same local segment. But the Internet is a network of networks — connecting billions of heterogeneous devices across continents. That is the realm of the **Network Layer (Layer 3)**.

---

## Pedagogical Philosophy

<Callout type="info" title="The Golden Rule of IP Routing">
A switch delivers packets based on **identity** (who you are, your 48-bit MAC address). A router delivers packets based on **location** (where you are, your hierarchical IP address and subnet mask). Just like a postal letter requires a country, zip code, and street before reaching an apartment door, routing enables packets to traverse hundreds of intermediate hops without any router having to memorize every host on Earth.
</Callout>

In this stage, you will master:
* **Binary Math & Subnetting:** Understanding bit boundaries, powers of 2, CIDR slash notation (`/24`, `/16`, `/29`), network IDs, and broadcast addresses.
* **Address Exhaustion Solutions:** DHCP dynamic lease negotiation (DORA), IPv4 NAT/PAT port address translation, and modern 128-bit IPv6 addressing.
* **Hands-On CLI Configuration:** Navigating Cisco IOS User EXEC, Privileged EXEC, and Global Config modes; configuring IP interfaces; and device hardening.
* **Forwarding Logic:** Longest Prefix Match (LPM), administrative distances, routing table lookup, TTL decrements, and static routes.
