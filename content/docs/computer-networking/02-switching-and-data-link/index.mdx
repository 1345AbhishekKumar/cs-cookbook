Welcome to **Stage 2: Data Link & Switching**. 

Once electricity and optical light can traverse physical cables, the next engineering challenge is arbitration: how do multiple machines share a single physical wire without talking over one another, and how do switches learn where to deliver frames using hardware MAC addresses?

---

## Pedagogical Philosophy

<Callout type="info" title="The Separation of Concerns">
The beauty of modern networking lies in **layer encapsulation**. An application sending a JSON payload does not need to know whether the physical medium is a Cat6 copper patch cord or a subsea fiber optic cable. The Data Link layer (Layer 2) provides local hop-by-hop framing, error detection, and switching, allowing higher layers to remain completely media-agnostic.
</Callout>

In this stage, you will master:
* **The Classical Architectural Models:** Comparing the 7-layer OSI academic model with the pragmatic 4-layer TCP/IP DOD model.
* **Encapsulation & Decapsulation:** How application data gets wrapped into Segments, Packets, Frames, and transmitted as Bits.
* **Ethernet & Switching Mechanics:** Framing, CRC FCS error detection, CSMA/CD historical collision detection vs full-duplex, switch CAM table learning, ARP broadcasts, and ICMP diagnostics.
* **Network Segmentation:** Isolating broadcast domains with 802.1Q VLANs and Wi-Fi 802.11 contention arbitration.
