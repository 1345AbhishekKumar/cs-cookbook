Welcome to your first networking milestone project: **The Campus Network Physical Architecture**.

In this capstone, you act as the Principal Infrastructure Architect for **TravelBuddy** as it moves into a 3-building corporate headquarters campus. You will design the physical media topology, calculate transceiver optical budgets, eliminate single points of failure, and draft the structural cabling plan.

---

## Architectural Problem Statement

TravelBuddy occupies three physical buildings across an 800-meter campus:
* **Building A (Headquarters):** 3 floors, 450 workstation drops, main on-prem datacenter rack.
* **Building B (Customer Support & Call Center):** 2 floors, 300 workstation drops, high VoIP traffic.
* **Building C (Logistics & Warehouse):** 1 large floor, industrial motorized machinery, RF interference, 80 drops + 20 Wi-Fi 7 APs.

## Engineering Design Requirements

<Steps>
<Step>
### Requirement 1: Optical Backbone Selection
* The distance between Building A and Building C is **750 meters**.
* **Decision:** Multi-Mode Fiber (OM4) maxes out around 400m–550m at 10 Gbps due to modal dispersion. Therefore, the inter-building conduit **must use OS2 Single-Mode Fiber (SMF)** with 1310 nm 10GBASE-LR optics.
</Step>

<Step>
### Requirement 2: Electrical Noise Immunity in Building C
* Building C has high-voltage conveyor motors and heavy electromagnetic interference (EMI).
* **Decision:** Traditional unshielded copper (UTP) will experience packet corruption and CRC errors. All workstation and access point drops in Building C must use **Cat6a S/FTP (Shielded/Foiled Twisted Pair)** with bonded chassis grounding.
</Step>

<Step>
### Requirement 3: Maximum 100-Meter Copper Drop Rule
* Horizontal cabling from telecom closets (IDF) to employee desks cannot exceed 90 meters of solid core horizontal cable plus 10 meters of combined patch cords (100m channel total).
* Building A requires dedicated IDF closets on Floor 1, Floor 2, and Floor 3, connected back to the MDF (Main Distribution Frame) via OM4 multi-mode fiber.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Optical Budget Calculation Verification

To ensure fiber links do not experience receiver saturation or attenuation loss:

$$
\text{Total Cable Loss (dB)} = (\text{Cable Length in km} \times \text{Fiber Attenuation per km}) + (\text{Splices} \times 0.1\text{ dB}) + (\text{Connectors} \times 0.5\text{ dB})
$$

For the 750m (0.75 km) OS2 run:
* $0.75\text{ km} \times 0.4\text{ dB/km (at 1310 nm)} = 0.3\text{ dB}$
* 2 patch panel connector pairs ($2 \times 0.5\text{ dB} = 1.0\text{ dB}$)
* 2 fusion splices ($2 \times 0.1\text{ dB} = 0.2\text{ dB}$)
* **Total Link Loss:** $1.5\text{ dB}$ (Well within the $6.0\text{ dB}$ dynamic power budget of 10GBASE-LR transceivers).
