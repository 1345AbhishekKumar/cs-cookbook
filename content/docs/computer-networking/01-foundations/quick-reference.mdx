## Copper Twisted-Pair Standards (Ethernet)

| Standard | Max Bandwidth | Max Distance | Shielding | Primary Production Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cat5e** | 1 Gbps (1,000 Mbps) | 100 meters | UTP | Legacy office drops, basic VoIP phones |
| **Cat6** | 1 Gbps (10 Gbps < 55m) | 100 m / 55 m | UTP / STP | Standard modern commercial workstation drops |
| **Cat6a** | 10 Gbps | 100 meters | STP / FTP | Modern enterprise floors, PoE+ cameras/APs |
| **Cat7** | 10 Gbps | 100 meters | S/FTP | Datacenter top-of-rack copper interconnects |
| **Cat8** | 25 Gbps / 40 Gbps | 30 meters | Shielded | Ultra-short switch-to-server DAC/RJ45 runs |

## Optical Fiber Comparison: Single-Mode vs. Multi-Mode

| Parameter | Multi-Mode Fiber (MMF - OM3/OM4/OM5) | Single-Mode Fiber (SMF - OS2) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Diameter** | 50 µm | 9 µm |
| **Light Source** | Inexpensive VCSEL (850 nm / 1300 nm) | High-precision Laser (1310 nm / 1550 nm) |
| **Modal Dispersion** | High (multiple light reflection paths) | Extremely Low (single beam trajectory) |
| **Max Reach** | 300 m – 550 m (at 10G/40G) | 10 km – 80 km+ (thousands of km with EDFAs) |
| **Primary Use** | Datacenter campus intra-building, rack-to-rack | Subsea ocean links, metro telco WAN backbone |
| **Jacket Color** | Aqua (OM3/OM4) or Lime Green (OM5) | Yellow (OS1/OS2) |

## Geographic Network Classifications

| Scope | Full Name | Geographic Reach | Dominant Transmission Tech |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PAN** | Personal Area Network | 1 – 10 meters | Bluetooth (BLE), Zigbee, Ultra-Wideband (UWB) |
| **LAN** | Local Area Network | 1 room – 1 building | Ethernet (802.3), Wi-Fi 6/7 (802.11ax/be) |
| **CAN** | Campus Area Network | University / Corporate site | Multi-mode optical fiber inter-building links |
| **MAN** | Metropolitan Area Network | Citywide | Dark Fiber, Metro Ethernet (Carrier Ethernet) |
| **WAN** | Wide Area Network | Countrywide / Global | Multiprotocol Label Switching (MPLS), Internet IPsec, BGP |
| **SAN** | Storage Area Network | Datacenter server room | Fibre Channel (FC), iSCSI, NVMe-over-Fabrics (NVMe-oF) |

## Physical Topologies Comparison

| Topology | Fault Tolerance | Cabling Complexity | Cost | Bottleneck / Single Point of Failure (SPOF) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bus** | Terrible (cable cut kills bus) | Low | Low | Main coax tap & terminator |
| **Star** | High for hosts | Moderate | Moderate | Central switch / hub |
| **Ring** | Low (unless dual counter-rotating) | Moderate | Moderate | Cable break halts token passing |
| **Full Mesh** | Absolute maximum ($N(N-1)/2$ links) | Exponential | Prohibitive | None (full link redundancy) |
| **Spine-and-Leaf Clos** | Extremely High (ECMP multi-path) | High | Enterprise | Non-blocking east-west datacenter fabric |

## Physical Constraints & Formulas

* **Speed of Light in Vacuum ($c$):** $300,000\text{ km/s}$ ($3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$).
* **Speed of Light in Optical Silica Glass:** $\approx 200,000\text{ km/s}$ ($c / 1.47$).
* **Fiber Latency Rule of Thumb:** $\approx 5\ \mu\text{s}$ per kilometer of fiber round-trip.
* **Maximum Ethernet Collision Domain (Copper):** 100 meters without active regeneration.
